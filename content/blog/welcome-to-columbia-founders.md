## Building a home for founder stories

Welcome to the Columbia Founders blog. This space is designed for practical perspectives from founders, operators, investors, and members of the Columbia community.

We will use it to publish:

- Founder lessons and tactical playbooks
- Community announcements and event recaps
- Member stories from across the Columbia ecosystem

If you have an idea for a future post, draft it as Markdown in `content/blog` and include the frontmatter fields documented in the README.
